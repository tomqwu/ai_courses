  • That cost makes a subscription honest

  • Evidence first, then the model

AI Product Studio / M7 · Monetize/Slide transcript/14 / 28

  • The observed effect: the executive's question changes
  • "why should I believe you?" becomes "when can we start?"

AI Product Studio / M7 · Monetize/Slide transcript/22 / 28
This is the strongest evidence in the module and it comes from a real product's own files: `course/00-research/03-ai-qe-deep-read.md` §1 and §3.
