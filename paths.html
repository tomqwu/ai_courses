What a unit is

Every module in this course is built the same way, and the path pages show it:

  1. Introduction — the cover and the module's objectives
  2. Lesson — three teaching segments, 27 across the course
  3. Exercise — one hands-on lab per module
  4. Knowledge check — eight questions per module, 72 across the course
  5. Summary — the recap and the discussion prompt

Every duration on these pages is measured from the recorded narration, not estimated. Lab times are quoted from each module's own source, because a lab is hours of hands-on work and its narration is a single slide.
