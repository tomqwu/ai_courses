## 3 · The problem — and the stakes

You have expertise. What you don't have is a product that survives a skeptical reader.

Three failures kill expert content before it sells. **Unlabeled numbers** — a task-level speedup presented as a budget saving, which the AI × QE claim taxonomy names as "the most common error in AI business cases" (`ai_qe/docs/principles.md`). **No provenance** — a claim with no retrieval date, sample, or method, so the buyer's cheapest objection (belief) stays open. **No route** — one 40-slide deck that serves neither the executive who decides nor the engineer who verifies, so the decision stalls.

The stake is not vanity. Every one of those failures converts a qualified buyer into a polite decline, and you never learn which one did it. The fix is not more confidence. It is a **record**: research logged before it ships, sources hashed, claims labeled, limits published, editions frozen.

## 5 · The proof behind the method

This bundle is built on a public case study you can open right now. **AI × QE** (`github.com/tomqwu/ai_qe`) is a research-backed briefing site with a consulting funnel:

- **116 narrated slides across 4 decks** — 21 + 33 + 26 + 36 (`ai_qe/_data/briefing_room.json`).
- **A published 14-finding self-audit** — four high priority, nine medium, one lower — with a remediation table mapping finding → response → verification (`ai_qe/research/reviews/site-audit-2026-09-06.md`, `ai_qe/research/reviews/remediation-2026-09-06.md`).
- **SHA-256 provenance manifests** that honestly log a failed retrieval as `"status": "unavailable"` (`ai_qe/research/document-manifest.json`).
- **Editioned immutable releases** separating site, slide, questionnaire, and research editions (`ai_qe/_data/release.yml`, `ai_qe/releases.md`).
- **A capped phased pilot design** with sponsor-signed go/no-go gates, 10%/15% effort bands, and ≥30 comparable tasks per arm (`ai_qe/_data/pilot_gates.json`, `ai_qe/docs/method/phased-pilot.md`).

**What that case study is not.** The ai_qe funnel stages are planned and recorded stages, not delivered client results. The site publishes that limit itself: "Planning inputs and proposed outcomes are not observed client results" (`ai_qe/README.md`), and its engagement record states "No client price or start date has been agreed" (`ai_qe/_data/engagement.json`). You will learn to write that sentence about your own product — because it is the sentence that makes the rest credible.

Your course proof assets are the same kind: a provenance table, a published audit, cited slides. No badges you cannot open.
