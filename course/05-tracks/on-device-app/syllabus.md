# Syllabus — On-Device AI Apps (Track 1)

> 6 weeks · self-paced · ~22–28 hours total · $199
> Weekly rhythm: one lesson block (60–90 min) → one lab (2–3 h) → one 8-question quiz → one dated evidence entry.
> **The rule that governs every week:** every action step ends in a commit, a test run, or a post; every lab ends in a verifiable artifact (`course/01-design/content-standards.md`, §0).

## Recurring deliverables (every week)

- **Evidence entry** in the Lab M1 format — commands with results, environment, revision, and at least one honest limitation (`course/03-content/m01-operating-system/lesson.md`, M1.3). The week's lab is not done until this exists.
- **Loop journal line** — one line per Spec-to-Ship stage you touched that week (`course/03-content/m00-orientation/lesson.md`, M0.2).
- **Community post** — each lab ships a discussion prompt; posting it is the +14-point completion lever the parent course is designed around (`course/00-research/02-course-market-research.md`, §B).

## How this track is assessed

| Component | Weight | How it is judged |
|---|---|---|
| Labs M0–M3 | Pass/fail gate | Objective acceptance checklists; a lab is passed only when every box is verifiable. Red runs must be recorded before green runs. |
| Quizzes M0–M3, M7 | Pass/fail gate | 8 auto-gradable questions each, answer keys included (40 questions total). Target ≥6/8 per quiz; retake freely. |
| Week 6 launch deliverables | Pass/fail gate | Sourced pricing worksheet, positioning one-liner, packaging page, 8-section sales page, 5-email mini-arc, tagged public repo, 5-minute demo. |

This track does **not** include the parent course's scored capstone (`course/03-content/m08-launch-capstone/lab.md`) or its certificate threshold. The Week 6 close is a scaled ship-and-demo checkpoint, judged against the Lab M7 checklist and the M8 four-beat demo structure — not the five-dimension capstone rubric from `course/01-design/assessment-and-rubrics.md`.
