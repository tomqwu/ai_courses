# Handout M8 — Launch: Sales Page, Email Arc, Capstone

**Mental model:** the page is an eight-move sequence sized by price; the arc earns trust before it spends it; the capstone is one archetype through the whole Spec-to-Ship Loop, with evidence.

## Keep these seven

1. **Page skeleton:** headline → for/isn't → stakes → outcomes → bio → testimonials → FAQ → pricing + one CTA.
2. **Revenue model:** `list × open × click × page conversion × price` (1,200 × 38% × 15% × 16% × $797 ≈ $8,767).
3. **Arc:** 3 warmup (origin, proof, free tool) + 4 conversion (cart open, objections, proof, final call).
4. **Email template:** one idea, one CTA, subject line for a technical skeptic, no emoji.
5. **Deliverability checklist:** SPF + DKIM + DMARC; opt-in only; scrub hard bounces; test to Gmail and corporate.
6. **Evidence record:** commands with counts → artifact links (repo at a tag, spec folder, evidence log, sales page) → limitations/not-verified.
7. **Demo beats:** problem 30s → loop walkthrough 2 min → live demo 2 min → evidence + limits 30s.

## Three gotchas

1. **Topics instead of outcomes in section 4.** "Week 3 covers privacy" is a syllabus; "you'll ship a fail-closed local-only mode" is a claim.
2. **A warmup email that sells.** One link in email 2 costs the open rate on email 7.
3. **A deadline you extend.** The next launch's final 48 hours are paid for by the deadline you kept this time.
