Launch: Sales Page, Email Arc, Capstone

turn shipped work into a sellable page, a launch arc, and a demo.

Learning objectives

  • Write a converting sales page in eight evidence-backed sections.
  • Source every proof claim from artifacts you already own.
  • Run a seven-email arc with deliverability configured first.
  • Model launch revenue, then record actuals honestly.
  • Ship v1 through the full Spec-to-Ship Loop.
  • Deliver a five-minute demo that ends on limits.

Modules 1–7 (earlier labs are the capstone's raw material — reuse and extend them; do not restart)
